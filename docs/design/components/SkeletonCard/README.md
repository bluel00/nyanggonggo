`SkeletonCard` is the loading placeholder in the card's shape: a `surface` card with `shadow-card-ended`, a 1:1 block and three text lines on `status-ended-bg`, no shimmer and no entrance effect. The lines sit in boxes 24 / 20 / 16 high with 2px between them, padded 10 on top and 12 at the sides and bottom; each bar is centred in its box: 14px at 100% (radius 7), 12px at 60% (radius 6), 12px at 50% (radius 6). So the gaps are 15 / 11 / 8 above the bars and 14 below the last (code `skeleton-card.tsx`, #84, PR #85). Render 3 for the first load, and 1 at the end of the feed while the next page loads; there is no end-of-list message. The detail page has its own skeleton with the same structure and sizes as the detail (photo box, title row 32px, dot row, info lines, CTA placeholders), and it shows the back button; before hydration that button is a link to `/`.
