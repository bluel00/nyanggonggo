`SkeletonCard` is the loading placeholder in the card's shape: a `surface` card with `shadow-card-ended`, a 1:1 block and three text lines on `status-ended-bg`, with the loading shimmer (`shimmer-duration` 1400ms; none under reduced motion) and no entrance effect. The lines sit in boxes 24 / 20 / 16 high with 2px between them, padded 10 on top and 12 at the sides and bottom; each bar is centred in its box: 14px at 100% (radius 7), 12px at 60% (radius 6), 12px at 50% (radius 6). So the gaps are 15 / 11 / 8 above the bars and 14 below the last (code `skeleton-card.tsx`, #84, PR #85). Render 3 for the first load, and 1 at the end of the feed while the next page loads; there is no end-of-list message. The detail page has its own skeleton with the same structure and sizes as the detail (photo box, title row 32px, dot row, info lines, CTA placeholders), and it shows the back button; before hydration that button is a link to `/`. See the Screens captures 12 and 13.

- **Loading character (2026-10-30, #111).** On a cat or dog list's first page (first load or a filter change), if the page has not arrived after `loading-character-delay` (500ms), the first SkeletonCard's photo block shows the species' character (96px, centred) with "공고를 불러오고 있어요" (`body`, `text-2`) 10px under it, fading in over `loading-character-fade` (200ms). The shimmer keeps running under it. It goes with the skeleton when the page arrives. Not on other-species lists, the ended filter list, 관심 공고, the detail skeleton or the next-page card. Reduced motion: the still character at 500ms, no fade, no shimmer. See `characters.md`.
- **Photo loading.** A card's photo block, and the detail photo box, keep the same shimmer on `status-ended-bg` until that photo has loaded; each one stops on its own. A failed photo shows lucide `ImageOff`, without shimmer.
