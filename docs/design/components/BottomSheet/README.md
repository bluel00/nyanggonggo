`BottomSheet` slides up inside the 480px column (in the app: `shared/ui/sheet`, a Radix Dialog): dim, `surface` sheet with `radius-card` top corners. Consumer provides `open`, `onClose`, `title`, `children` and optional `footer`. Two variants: with a footer (filter sheet) and without one (shelter info sheet). It is absolutely positioned in `.cn-column` (position: relative), not fixed to the viewport. Closing without applying discards the draft values.

- **With footer (filter sheet): three parts.** Head (handle + title "필터"), a body that scrolls, and a fixed footer holding the 적용하기 `Button variant="apply"` (the only place that variant is used). The footer has the sheet's own `surface` background with `shadow-bar` on its top edge; its padding is `space-3` (12px) on top, `space-4` (16px) at the sides, and `space-4` + env(safe-area-inset-bottom) at the bottom. Max height `min(85%, 85svh)`, so 적용하기 is always visible on small screens (375×667 included).
- **Filter content.** Sections in order: 축종 (`Segmented`, 고양이 / 강아지 / 기타 동물), 지역 (two `Select`s: 시/도 and 시/군/구), 보호 상태 (`Chip`s), 성별 (`Chip`s), 정렬 (`Radio`s). Section labels `body` + `text-2`.
- **Without footer (shelter info sheet).** Handle + title (the shelter name) + content (`ShelterInfo`), no fixed bottom area and no button at the bottom. The body's bottom padding is `space-6` (24px) + env(safe-area-inset-bottom).
- **Closing** (both): a tap on the dim, a tap on the handle ("닫기"), or Esc. No drag-to-close.
- Motion: 250ms `ease-out`. Reduced motion: no open/close animation (the app matches this since 2026-10-27, #61).
