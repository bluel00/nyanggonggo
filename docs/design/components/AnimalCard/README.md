`AnimalCard` is the whole-card link to a detail page: a white `surface` card with `radius-card` (22) and `shadow-card` floating on the page gradient, a 1:1 photo (`object-fit: cover`, `object-position: center`), the status badge top-left on the photo (`photo-pill`), an optional `action` top-right on the photo, then three lines. Consumer provides a Domain `animal` ({id, species, regionText, shelterName, foundPlaceText, noticeNoText, status, noticeEndAt, endResultText, images…}), `now`, `onClick` or `href`, and `action` (ReactNode). The card works out D-N from `noticeEndAt` and `now`; there is no `dDay` field (code `animal.ts`, `animal-card.tsx`). This design system's mock data still carries a `dDay` number to stand in for that result.

- **Lines.**
  1. Title: the region ("가나시 다라구"; other species "토끼 · 가나시 다라구"), `card-title`. The Domain has no name.
  2. The shelter name, or the found place when there is none, `body` + `text-2`.
  3. "공고 번호 가나-가나-2026-00001", `caption` + `text-2`, one line with an ellipsis (`data-slot="notice-no"`). Left out when there is no number. The card grows by this line. Whether the card keeps it is decided after watching it in use.
- **Badge.** "보호중 · 마감 D-5", on the last day "보호중 · 오늘 마감". Deadline D-3 or less keeps the words and changes only the colour (`status-near-deadline*`). Ended: "종료 · <결과>", or "종료". See `StatusBadge`.
- **Action slot.** The card takes only `action`; the widget (`widgets/animal-list`, `widgets/favorite-list`) passes `FavoriteButton variant="overlay"` (`still` for cards that do not move), because entities do not know features. In this design system's bundle the same form is `FavoriteButton onPhoto` (`calm` on ended cards). The slot is a sibling of the card link, never nested in it, so pressing the heart neither presses the card nor opens the detail. Both the list and the 관심 공고 list show the heart.
- **Press.** `press-scale` and `shadow-card-pressed` over `press-duration`, only when the card link is pressed.
- **Ended cards.** saturate(0.7) on the photo only, no dim, `shadow-card-ended`, and no press, entrance or transition effect; the heart only toggles its fill.
- **Card → detail.** Only list (`/`) cards run the transition (see README motion table). 관심 공고 cards open the detail at once. If the tapped card's photo has not loaded yet, the detail opens straight to its skeleton.
- **Photo loading.** Until its photo has loaded, the 1:1 block is `status-ended-bg` with the loading shimmer, on every card including ended ones (it says loading, it is not decoration). Each card's block stops on its own. See `SkeletonCard`.
- Reduced motion: no effect. Never pass DTO fields or contact numbers to the card. Nothing else goes on the photo.
