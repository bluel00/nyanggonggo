## 원칙

꾸밈 규칙보다 먼저 온다. 아래와 부딪히는 꾸밈은 넣지 않는다.

- **사진은 덮거나 칠하지 않는다.** 사진 위에는 지금 있는 상태 배지(`photo-pill`), 목록·관심 목록 카드의 관심 하트 원, 상세의 뒤로가기(`Button variant="overlay"`)만 둔다. 그림자·그라데이션·후광·광택·반투명 바탕 같은 새 꾸밈은 사진 바깥에만 둔다. 화면 전환 중에도 사진이 반투명한 바탕 아래 비치지 않게 한다. 종료 공고의 `saturate(0.7)`는 기존 규칙으로 유지한다.
- **종료 공고는 차분하게 둔다.** 장식·튐·등장 효과를 넣지 않는다. 종료 카드는 누름 효과도, 목록 등장 효과도, 상세로 갈 때 전환 효과도 없이 바로 바뀌고, 그림자는 `shadow-card-ended`로 납작하다. 종료 상세에는 캐릭터·사진 그림자·마감 글자가 없다.
- **목록은 빠르게 둔다.** 첫 화면에서 한 번만 움직이고 스크롤 중에는 효과가 없다. 스크롤로 새로 보이는 카드, 다음 페이지는 그냥 보인다. 상세는 목록 위에 얹는 층이라 닫아도 목록이 다시 그려지지 않는다. 필터를 바꿔 뼈대가 다시 보이면 첫 화면 등장도 다시 한 번 돈다.
- **움직임 줄이기 설정이면 모든 효과 없이 바로 바뀐다.** 전환·등장·하트·누름·시트·캐릭터 모두. 대신 페이드도 넣지 않는다. (시트도 2026-10-27부터 코드가 이 규칙을 따른다.)

서비스명은 "멍냥공고"다(옛 이름 냥공고). 이름과 한 줄 소개는 코드에서 `SERVICE_NAME`, `SERVICE_TAGLINE` 한 곳에서만 정의한다. 문서 `<title>`(홈 "오늘은 누구를 보러 왔어요? | 멍냥공고", 관심 목록 "관심 공고 | 멍냥공고", 상세 "<지역> <축종> 공고 | 멍냥공고"(예: "가나시 다라구 고양이 공고 | 멍냥공고", 기타 축종은 축종 자리에 동물 이름), 목록과 그 밖의 화면은 사이트 기본 "멍냥공고"), OG 이미지(공고 없음), 로고 그림의 접근성 이름이 이 상수를 읽는다. 홈 위의 이름은 글자가 아니라 Logo 가로 조합 그림(line, 높이 56)이고, 그 아래 한 줄 소개 "보호 중인 아이들의 공고가 / 더 많은 사람에게 닿도록."이 온다. 카카오 피드 카드는 제목이 "<지역> <축종>"이고 썸네일이 대표 사진(없을 때만 OG 이미지)이라 보통 서비스명이 들어가지 않는다. 화면 타이틀은 서비스명이 아니라 축종에 따라 "고양이 공고" / "강아지 공고" / "기타 동물 공고"다.

## Content fundamentals

Write in short, warm Korean (해요체). The product is a photo feed, not an adoption service: the action is sharing, never adopting. Protected notices keep the shelter's address and phone in the shelter info sheet, opened from [보호소 정보] on the detail's 보호소 row; the sheet has small [지도] (map search, new tab) and [전화] (`tel:`) buttons. Ended notices have no [보호소 정보] and keep the shelter name. The person in charge is never shown. Real copy: "지금은 고양이를 불러오지 못했어요" (list error), "조건에 맞는 고양이가 없어요 / 필터를 바꿔서 다시 찾아보세요" (list empty), "지금은 공고를 불러오지 못했어요" (detail error), "관심 공고" (favourites title and header link), "아직 관심 공고가 없어요 / 하트를 눌러 관심 공고에 담아 보세요" (favourites empty), "지금은 관심 공고를 불러오지 못했어요" (favourites error), "관심 공고에 담기" / "관심 공고에서 빼기" (heart), "관심 공고에 담았어요" / "관심 공고에서 뺐어요" (toasts), "일부 공고를 불러오지 못했어요" + [다시 시도] (favourites partial error), "링크가 복사됐어요". Sharing has two buttons in the detail CTA bar: "카카오톡 공유" (the primary) and a second one that says what it will do: "다른 앱으로 공유" when the OS share sheet can open (it opens the sheet), or "링크 복사" where it cannot, as in some desktop browsers (it copies the link and shows "링크가 복사됐어요"). Ended notices keep both. Card badge: "보호중 · 마감 D-5", on the last day "보호중 · 오늘 마감"; a deadline of D-3 or less changes only the colour (no 임박 text). Detail: badge "보호중" with "공고 마감 D-5" / "오늘 마감" next to it. The ended badge reads "종료 · <결과>" (입양, 반환, 자연사 … as the API writes it), or "종료" without one. Outside the app (OG image, share) the deadline is a date: share and link-preview description "성별 · 나이 · 보호중 · 공고 마감 11월 3일(화) · <특이사항>" (no shelter name). Sex and age read "암컷 · 2026년생 · 60일 미만", or "성별 미상" / "나이 미상". Sort: "최신순" / "공고 마감 빠른순". List copy names the current species (고양이 / 강아지 / 기타 동물); screens that mix species or don't know it (favourites, detail error) say 공고 instead. There is no emoji in the UI copy (the 🐾 in the old favourites empty state is gone). The feed never says it has ended; it keeps showing `SkeletonCard`.

## Visual foundations

Direction A "햇살 담요" (chosen 2026-10-14; source: the design "냥공고 홈 · 축종 선택", boards DirA, HomeA and MotionA; where the board's table and the demo differed, the demo's played values are the rule). Warm and soft, and the photo is still the hero.

Ground: a vertical gradient `bg` (#FFF8EE) → `bg-end` (#F9EBD9) on the non-scrolling column, so it spans the screen, not the scroll length. White `surface` cards float on it with `shadow-card` (two warm-brown layers); pressed cards drop to `shadow-card-pressed`; ended cards stay flat with `shadow-card-ended`. Card radius `radius-card` (22), home choice cards `radius-choice` (24) with a radial `choice-halo` → `choice-halo-end` circle (104px) behind each character. All text is checked on `bg-end`, the darker end: `text` 13.4:1, `text-2` 5.6:1, status `-text` 4.5:1 or more.

Type: body stays Pretendard with `title` (20/700), `card-title` (16/600), `body` (14/400), `caption` (12/400) and `badge` (12/600). Jua (배달의민족 주아, SIL OFL 1.1, 400 only) is used only for fixed strings: `display-home` 28/36 for the home question, `display-title` 22/28 for the list title (고양이 공고 / 강아지 공고 / 기타 동물 공고) and the 고양이 / 강아지 / 기타 동물 choice labels, `display-logo` 20/28 for the wordmark only in the design system's `Logo` lockup preview (in the app the home name is the Logo lockup picture, not Jua text; the `logo` colour token was removed 2026-10-27). It ships as one subset font holding only the glyphs of the 7 fixed strings 오늘은 누구를 보러 왔어요?, 고양이, 강아지, 고양이 공고, 강아지 공고, 기타 동물 공고, 기타 동물 (see `handoff.md` (d) for how the subset is built); never the full Korean font, and never for text that comes from data. The detail title of a protected notice comes from the public API's 기관 이름 (`orgNm`), so its glyphs cannot be known ahead: it is `display-detail` in Pretendard 24/32/700. The ended-notice title is Pretendard `title` (20/700) drawn with a 32px line height, so the title row is 32px in every state and nothing below it moves when the skeleton is replaced. Error and empty-state titles are `card-title` (16/600), so they read calm. Adding a new fixed string in Jua means rebuilding the subset.

Buttons and controls: only the filter sheet's 적용하기 is a peach gradient pill (`Button variant="apply"`: `apply-from` → `apply-to`, `on-apply`, `shadow-apply`). The default primary Button stays `text`-filled for the one action of a system screen ("다시 시도"); pressed it is `primary-pressed`. Row actions such as [보호소 정보], [지도] and [전화] are small text buttons (`Button variant="small-action"`): `text-2` caption with a 14px icon, 44×44 hit area, the row height unchanged. FavoriteButton and the filter button are `surface` with `shadow-control`, no outline; when a sex is chosen the filter button takes the selected Chip look (`text` fill) and its accessible name becomes "필터, 암컷"; Chips are `surface` with `shadow-chip`, selected Chips are `text`-filled. ShareButton keeps `kakao` / `on-kakao` and takes the remaining width; the second share button (`ShareButton variant="other"`) is a content-wide white pill like FavoriteButton (14px label + 18px icon). The detail CTA bar is `bg` with `shadow-bar` on its top edge.

Detail photo: the box takes the first photo's ratio clamped to 3:4–4:3, inset 24px each side, `radius-card`, contain on `bg`, with `shadow-photo` around it (none on ended notices). Back sits on the photo's top-left as the existing overlay button; the detail skeleton and the detail error show it too. The dot row under the photo (12px above + 6px dots) is always reserved, empty for a single photo, so nothing below moves.

Detail info rows: 발견 장소 → 보호소 [보호소 정보] → 공고 번호 → 공고 기간 → 종료 사유 → 특이사항, empty ones left out. 주소 and 전화 live in the shelter info sheet (a bottom sheet without a fixed footer: 주소 [지도] → 전화 [전화]). Values wrap only at spaces (`keep-all`) and break anywhere when there are none (`overflow-wrap: anywhere`). Ended: no [보호소 정보], 종료 사유 shown (and 특이사항 dropped when it is the same). See `DetailInfo` and `ShelterInfo`. List cards have three lines: region, shelter (or found place), "공고 번호 <번호>".

Spacing is unchanged: page padding `space-4` (16), card gap `space-3` (12), touch target `touch` (44).

Colour points are for status only: 보호중 uses `status-protected` (dot) with `status-protected-bg` and `status-protected-text`; a deadline of D-3 or less uses `status-near-deadline` with `-bg` and `-text` (colour only, no separate words); 종료 uses `status-ended` with `-bg` and `-text`. Text never uses the point colours, only the `-text` variants, which meet 4.5:1. Exceptions to "accent = status": `kakao` with `on-kakao` on ShareButton; the `apply-from` → `apply-to` peach on 적용하기 and on the logo tile (see `handoff.md` (g)). The character illustrations use their own illustration-only palette, and the cat's bow (`#9C3548`) is a second, character-only exception; see the 캐릭터 section (`characters.md`). Over a photo, badges are `photo-pill` (white 90%) with a coloured dot. The filled favourite heart is `text`, never a colour.

Photos in list cards are 1:1, `object-fit: cover`, `object-position: center` (a 4:5 crop cut the head or face off about 1 in 10 cards, mostly landscape photos with the animal near an edge; 1:1 cuts about 1 in 35). The loading card (`SkeletonCard`) has the same 1:1 photo block, so cards do not jump when the photos arrive. The OG image stays 4:5, `object-fit: cover`, `object-position: center 35%`. On the detail page photos are never cropped: the photo box takes the first photo's width/height ratio clamped to 3:4–4:3, and every photo in the carousel is `contain` inside that box on `bg`, so the box height stays the same while swiping. If the first photo's size is not known in time, the box is 1:1. Shelter photos are about half 4:3 landscape and a third 3:4 portrait, so a fixed 4:5 cover hid about 40% of a landscape photo. The full-screen viewer shows the original ratio (`contain`) on `viewer-bg`. Ended animals: photo saturate(0.7), grey badge, no dim.

Layout: design frame 390x844 (safe area 47 top, 34 bottom). On desktop the content is one `column-max` (480px) column centred on `canvas`; there is no desktop layout. Bottom sheet, viewer and the fixed CTA bar live inside that column.

Detail as a layer: from the list or 관심 공고 the detail opens at `/animals/{id}` as a full-screen modal layer over the list, which stays where it was (no reload, no scroll restore needed). Back (button, browser, swipe) and Esc close only the layer; pulling down on the detail does not refresh. Focus moves to the detail title and returns to the tapped card on close. A shared link or a refresh shows the detail as a full page, and its back goes to `/` (home for first-time visitors, the remembered list otherwise); a share link does not change the remembered settings.

Characters may idle on screens without photos, react briefly to a press on screens with photos, and never appear in ended-notice contexts (see `characters.md`). Focus ring is a 2px `focus-ring` with 2px offset.

## Iconography

Icons are simple 1.8px-stroke outline glyphs (heart, close, back, chat bubble in `on-kakao`). The app uses lucide directly: Heart, SlidersHorizontal, X, ChevronLeft, ChevronDown, Info, MapPin and Phone (14px, small text buttons), ImageOff (missing or failed photo), and Share2 and Link (18px, the second share button). The `Icon` component in `bundle.js` draws placeholders for these (heart, filter, close, back, chevron-down, info, map-pin, phone, image-off, share, link), and the Kakao chat bubble is inline in `ShareButton`.

Real screens: the asset group **Screens** holds 15 captures of the app at main `d4a3f5d` (2026-10-27), the code the specs above follow. The shelter info sheet is not captured (it would show real phone numbers).
